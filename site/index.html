FAQ

What does IceBlox do?

IceBlox continuously scans license plates from your dashboard camera and checks them against a known watchlist of ICE vehicles. If there's a match, your community gets an instant push notification.

What information do you collect?

IceBlox is designed to be privacy first. No images or videos are ever stored or uploaded. No license plate numbers are sent to our servers. The system is built so that we do not and cannot track your location.

How much does it cost?

Free.

What phones are supported?

iPhone (iOS 17+) and Android (9.0+).

Can I report activity manually?

Yes. The app includes a built-in report form you can use to report ICE activity anytime, even without a plate match.
